Stop scrolling. You have three thousand unread messages, and that's not even the worst part.
Here's the truth: replying isn't the hard part. Deciding is.
Every day we jump between email, WhatsApp, Slack and Telegram, and every single one wants an answer right now.
So what actually works? Three things.
One: reply in batches, twice a day. Two: keep your answers short. Three: turn off every notification you don't need.
Before, I spent four hours a day in my inbox. After? Forty minutes.
Last week my friend Sarah texted me: "How do you reply so fast?"
Honestly, it's not talent. It's a system.
